I am an undergraduate student at [Shiv Nadar Institute of Eminence](https://snu.edu.in/), pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in **Computer Science and Engineering** with a specialization in **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** and a **Minor in Mathematics** (current cumulative GPA: **9.14 / 10.0**).

My research and engineering interests lie at the intersection of **Machine Learning**, **Distributed & Decentralized Systems**, and **Deep Learning Systems**. I am especially fascinated by how mathematical principles enable scalable, privacy-preserving, and observable intelligence.

---

### Research Interests

- **Distributed & Decentralized Machine Learning**: Extending privacy-preserving decentralized learning frameworks (such as HPE Swarm Learning) across diverse model families and classical estimators.
- **Deep Generative Models & Computer Vision**: Investigating adversarial and cycle-consistent image-to-image translation architectures (CycleGAN) for cross-domain synthesis without paired data.
- **ML Systems & Observability**: Designing evaluation platforms with framework-agnostic adapters for monitoring telemetry, semantic drift, and consistency in foundation models.
- **Mathematical Foundations of Computing**: Grounding practical machine learning in analysis, differential equations, optimization, and number theory.
