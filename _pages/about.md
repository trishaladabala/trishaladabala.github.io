I am an undergraduate student at [Shiv Nadar Institute of Eminence](https://snu.edu.in/), pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in **Computer Science and Engineering** with a specialization in **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** and a **Minor in Mathematics** (cumulative GPA: **9.14 / 10.0**).

As an undergraduate student researcher, I work at the intersection of **Machine Learning Theory**, **Generative Models**, **High-Dimensional Geometry**, and **Computational Biology**. I am especially motivated by using mathematical and geometric principles to build scalable, observable, and efficient learning systems.

---

### Research Interests

- **Continuous-Latent Flow Matching & Generative Models**: Investigating probability flow ODEs/SDEs, manifold trajectory retraction (Encoder-Cycle Projection), causal wave-clock schedules, and trajectory distillation for non-autoregressive language generation.
- **High-Dimensional Geometry & Vector Quantization**: Uncovering spectral coordinate anisotropy in foundation model embeddings (e.g., ESM-2), developing data-oblivious quantization codecs with closed-form Bussgang de-attenuation, and engineering SIMD-accelerated asymmetric distance search.
- **Computational Connectomics & Network Neuroscience**: Building out-of-core streaming graph analytics pipelines for whole-brain electron microscopy connectomes (FlyWire FAFB v783), auditing statistical null models, and analyzing chemospecific recurrent feedback in the insect central complex.
- **Decentralized & Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning**: Designing framework-independent parameter aggregation protocols to extend decentralized Swarm Learning to diverse classical estimators.

### Highlights & Research Projects

- **[QuantPPI Vector Quantization](/projects/#research%20projects)**: Identified extreme coordinate anisotropy ($\mathrm{PR}/D = 0.0012$) in protein embeddings; proved Support-Orthogonal Bleed Elimination and Analytical Bussgang De-Attenuation theorems; surpassed trained FAISS OPQ ($0.9993$ vs. $0.9968$ Recall@20@30) with zero calibration data.
- **[VectorWing Connectomics](/projects/#research%20projects)**: Built an out-of-core pipeline analyzing 16.8M synapses under $<380\text{ MB}$ RAM; identified that classical edge-swapping collapses physical reciprocity by $4.61\times$; formulated degree-stratified permutation null models; discovered a $2.53\times$–$5.92\times$ heterotypic $\text{ACh} \leftrightarrow \text{GABA}$ reciprocity inversion with 100% strict chemical polarity in the fly steering matrix.
- **[Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE)](/projects/#research%20projects)** — *Software Development Intern*: Extended HPE Swarm Learning to support Scikit-Learn models with framework-independent weight handling for decentralized, privacy-preserving machine learning.
- **Academic Honors**: Named to the **Dean's List for Academic Excellence** across four semesters (Monsoon 2023, Spring 2024, Monsoon 2025, Spring 2026).
