I am an undergraduate student at [Shiv Nadar Institute of Eminence](https://snu.edu.in/), pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in **Computer Science and Engineering** with a specialization in **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** and a **Minor in Mathematics** (cumulative GPA: **9.14 / 10.0**).

As an undergraduate student researcher, I work at the intersection of **Machine Learning Theory**, **Generative Models**, **High-Dimensional Geometry**, and **Computational Biology**. I am especially motivated by using mathematical and geometric principles to build scalable, observable, and efficient learning systems for biological and foundation model representations.

---

### Research Interests

- **Continuous-Latent Flow Matching & Generative Models**: Investigating probability flow ODEs/SDEs, manifold trajectory retraction (Encoder-Cycle Projection), causal wave-clock schedules, and trajectory distillation for non-autoregressive sequence generation.
- **High-Dimensional Geometry & Vector Quantization**: Uncovering spectral coordinate anisotropy in foundation model embeddings (e.g., ESM-2), developing data-oblivious quantization codecs with closed-form Bussgang de-attenuation, and engineering SIMD-accelerated asymmetric distance search.
- **Computational Biology & Neuroinformatics**: Analyzing whole-brain synaptic connectivity using the FlyWire FAFB v783 electron microscopy connectome (139,000+ neurons, 50M+ chemical synapses), formulating statistical null models, and dissecting chemospecific recurrent microcircuits in the insect navigation system.
